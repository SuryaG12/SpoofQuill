Method

How to read the number

Four signals add points: a reply-to that does not match the sender, link text that hides where it goes, urgent language, and a link that asks for payment or a login. When several of these appear in one message, a stacking bonus adds more points. Unrelated signs together are stronger evidence than any one alone.

Low 00 to 29

The message shows little or no evidence of phishing. Check unusual requests through a channel you already trust.

Suspicious 30 to 59

The message has one or more real warning signs. Confirm the sender before you reply, pay, or sign in.

High 60 to 100

Several signals point the same way. Treat the message as hostile and do not use its links.
